Gradfessor by Afridi The right student for the right professor

Open the professor's reply first. Select the reply text for a cleaner capture. The app will ask which professor it belongs to.

Use on a department's faculty list. Gradfessor reads each profile for you in the background (one page every few seconds) and ranks everyone against your profile.

Use on a program's admissions page: deadlines, IELTS/TOEFL/GRE, minimum GPA, fee and fee waiver are pulled out for you to check.

For a single faculty profile, lab page or Google Scholar page. Select text first to capture only that part.
