    Professor matching

    Add professors you are considering. Paste their faculty page or Google Scholar text, or use the extension button on their page. Each match shows why it scored the way it did.

    Rank a whole department

    1. Open the department's Faculty or People page in Chrome.
    2. Click the Gradfessor icon, then Scan whole department.
    3. Gradfessor reads every profile in the background, finds each professor's recent papers (free, via OpenAlex) and ranks them here. You don't open any profile yourself.
    Paper lookup settings

    Without a key you can look up about 100 professors a day. A free key from openalex.org raises that about 10×. It stays in this browser.

    Ranked matches

    Sorted by overall score

    Scores compare the words in your profile with the professor's listed interests and paper titles. Four parts: topic fit 30%, evidence overlap 30%, methods 25%, research style 15%. They show where to look, not your chance of admission. Always read the papers yourself.

      AI prompt pack

      Build one complete prompt with your facts, the professor's papers and keywords, the document rules and plain-writing rules. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini (free versions work) and ask for the PDF. Then paste the result back into the Writing coach to check it.

      Use it
      ChatGPT Claude Gemini
      1. Copy, open an AI chat and paste.
      2. Answer its questions honestly.
      3. Download the PDF/Word file it makes (free plans may only give text; then paste into Word or Google Docs and export PDF).
      4. Read every line. Rewrite the [MY OWN SENTENCE] parts yourself.
      5. Paste the final text into the Writing coach for a last check.

      Start here

      Five steps from "I don't know which professors to email" to a report for every university you apply to. Free, and everything stays on your computer.

      1. 1

        Build your profile

        Upload your CV (PDF or Word). Gradfessor suggests your degrees, GPA, IELTS/TOEFL/GRE, skills and courses; you approve them. Add coursework with grades, experience and links.

      2. 2

        Rank a whole department

        Open a department's Faculty or People page, click the Gradfessor icon, then Scan whole department. Every professor is read, their recent papers are found (free, via OpenAlex), and they are ranked against your profile.

      3. 3

        Capture program requirements

        Open the program's admissions page (deadlines, English requirements, fees). Click the Gradfessor icon, then Capture program requirements. Deadline, IELTS/TOEFL/Duolingo minimums, GRE policy, minimum GPA, fee, fee waiver and letters are filled in for you to check, and compared with your scores.

      4. 4

        Write to the right professors

        Use Draft email on your top matches, write the one personal sentence, and open it in Gmail. Use the Writing coach and AI prompt pack for your SOP, CV and research statement.

      5. 5

        Download a report for each university

        Get an Excel workbook and a Word report per university: programs and requirements, whether you meet them, best-fit professors with papers to read, outreach status and a checklist.

      Try it with the demo first

      Load a fictional student, three professors and a program to see every feature. Your own data is replaced, so do this before you enter your real profile (or back up first).

      University reports

      Everything you know about each university in one place, compared with your profile. Download an Excel workbook or a Word report for any university, or one Excel file for all of them.

      Application tracker

      Deadlines, fees and waivers, required documents, professor emails, follow-ups and interviews in one place. Export it all to Excel any time.
