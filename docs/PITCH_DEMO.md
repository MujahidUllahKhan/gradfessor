# Live demo script for the NMSU Bold Idea pitch (about 3 minutes)

Opening line: "Professors get hundreds of irrelevant emails; students spend months writing them. Gradfessor shows both sides the fit before anyone hits Send. Grad student plus professor: Gradfessor, the right student for the right professor."

Before you go on stage: open `extension/app.html` in Chrome (or the installed extension), click **Delete my data** so it starts clean, and keep a second tab open on any faculty profile page. Zoom the browser to 125% so the room can read it.

1. **Profile (30 s).** Click **Load demo data**. "This is Sara, the student from the slides. She enters her education, GPA, IELTS bands, GRE, interests, skills and projects once." Point at the profile strength score and one tip.

2. **Matching (60 s).** Go to **Professors**. "Three professors from the same department. Dr. Chen scores 92, Dr. Rivera 47, Dr. Patel 0." Open **Why this score** on Dr. Chen: shared keywords, gap keywords, methods. "Every number is explainable. It is a research-fit guide, not an admission predictor."
   Then the wow moment: switch to a real department's faculty page, click the extension icon, **Scan whole department**. "Sara doesn't open 32 profiles. The extension reads them in the background and finds their papers for free." Switch back while the progress bar fills. (Run one scan the night before, so a finished ranking is already there if Wi-Fi is slow.)

3. **Email + writing coach (45 s).** Click **Draft email** on Dr. Chen. Show the three tones. Click **Edit this one**. The coach flags the one sentence Sara must write herself. Type a sentence and watch the red item disappear. "We make personal emails faster, not spam faster."

4. **Replies (15 s).** Open a sample reply in Gmail, click **Capture this email reply**. The app labels it "Enthusiastic" and suggests the next step.

5. **Tracker (30 s).** Go to **Tracker**. "Deadlines, fees and fee waivers, LORs, and every professor email. It already knows Sara's follow-up to Dr. Chen is due, counting business days."

6. **Prompt pack + Excel (20 s).** Show the AI prompt pack for an SOP ("works with free ChatGPT or Claude, asks for the PDF"), then click **Export Excel tracker** and open the file. "The dashboard is live formulas. Students can keep using it without the extension."

Close: "Free today, all on the student's device, zero server cost, so I can give it to every student in Pakistan. Paid later: hosted AI rewrites and cloud sync."

## Backup plan

If Wi-Fi fails, everything still works offline (fonts fall back to system fonts). Keep `templates/Gradfessor_Application_Tracker.xlsx` on the laptop in case Excel export is questioned.
