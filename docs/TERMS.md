# Terms of use (draft, v0.3)

Plain-language draft for the free version. Have it reviewed (for example by NMSU's Arrowhead Center or a lawyer) before a wide public launch.

1. **Free tool, no warranty.** Gradfessor by Afridi is provided "as is", without warranty, under the MIT License in this repository.
2. **Guidance only.** Match scores, reply labels and writing feedback are automated guides based on text overlap. They do not predict admission, funding or a professor's decision.
3. **Your data stays with you.** We do not operate a server or collect your data. You are responsible for backing it up (Backup button) and for your device's security.
4. **Your responsibility when you use it.** You decide what to send. Only claim skills and results you really have. Do not send mass or repeated emails. Follow each university's and website's rules.
5. **Third-party services.** Paper data comes from OpenAlex. Links open Google, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, Gmail or Outlook under their own terms. Gradfessor does not scrape Google Scholar, LinkedIn or email inboxes.
6. **AI tools.** The prompt pack is text you paste into an AI service of your choice. Follow each program's rules on AI use in applications; many ask applicants to confirm the work is their own.
7. **Changes.** These terms may change with new versions; the version in the repository applies.
