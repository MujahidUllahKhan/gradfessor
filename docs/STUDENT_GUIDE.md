# Gradfessor student guide

For students, and for anyone running a workshop (about 45 minutes with a live demo).

## Before you start

- A laptop with Google Chrome (Edge and Brave also work).
- Your CV as a PDF or Word file.
- The names of 2-3 universities and departments you are interested in.

## 1. Install (5 minutes)

1. Unzip `gradfessor-extension-v0.4.2.zip` into a folder you will keep, for example `Documents\Gradfessor`. Don't delete this folder later; Chrome runs the extension from it.
2. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder.
5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin **Gradfessor by Afridi**.
6. Click the **G✓** icon → **Open Gradfessor**. You will see **Start here**.

Tip: click **Load demo data** on the Start here page to see every feature with a fictional student first. Then click **Delete my data** before entering your own.

## 2. Build your profile (10 minutes)

Go to **My profile**.

1. Click **Upload CV** and choose your PDF or Word CV. It is read on your computer only.
2. Tick the suggestions you want (degrees and GPA, IELTS, GRE, skills, courses, publications) and click **Apply selected**. Check every value.
3. Fill what the CV did not have:
   - **Test scores:** all IELTS bands (programs often set a minimum per band), TOEFL sections, Duolingo, PTE, GRE, GRE Subject.
   - **Coursework:** your 5-8 strongest advanced courses with grades.
   - **Experience:** research assistantships, jobs, teaching; one line on what you did and one result.
   - **Research interests:** at least 5, specific ("stochastic inventory optimization", not "AI").
   - **Links:** LinkedIn, Google Scholar, GitHub.
4. Watch **Profile strength** rise. Each tip tells you what to add next.
5. Click **Done, lock my profile**. Your profile stays saved for all future applications; click **Edit profile** whenever you want to change something.
6. Optional: tick **Keep a copy in my Chrome account**. If you move to a new computer (signed into the same Chrome account) or reinstall the extension, Gradfessor offers to restore your profile.

Your data stays when you close Chrome or restart the computer. It is removed only if you click **Delete my data** or remove the extension, so click **Backup** once in a while (the app reminds you every two weeks).

Why it matters: interests, projects and papers drive the topic and evidence scores; skills and courses drive the Methods score; test scores and GPA drive the eligibility checks.

## 3. Rank a department (5 minutes + background time)

1. Open a department's **Faculty** or **People** page (for example "Industrial Engineering faculty" at your target university).
2. Click the **G✓** icon → **Scan whole department** → **Allow** access to that university's website.
3. Go to **Professors** in Gradfessor. Professors appear as they are read; their papers are looked up automatically.
4. Open **Why this score** on your top matches. Read the gap keywords: add them to your CV only if they are true for you.

If a scan finds nobody, open one professor's page and click **Capture this page as one professor** instead.

## 4. Capture program requirements (2 minutes per program)

1. Open the program's admissions or "how to apply" page (the one listing deadlines, English tests and fees).
2. Click the **G✓** icon → **Capture program requirements**.
3. In **Tracker**, check each value against the quote shown under it, then **Review and save as program**.
4. The Programs table shows **You vs. requirements**: green if you meet the minimum, red if not, orange for things to check (fee waivers, letters).

If the deadlines and test scores are on different pages, capture each page; save the second one onto the same program by editing it.

## 5. Contact the right professors

1. On a top match, click **Draft email**, pick a tone, and write the one sentence in [brackets] yourself.
2. Click **Open in Gmail**, send, then mark **Sent today** in the Tracker. Follow-ups are counted in business days.
3. When a professor replies, open the reply and click **Capture this email reply**. Gradfessor labels it and suggests the next step.
4. For SOP, CV or research statement: use **AI prompt pack**, paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, then check the result in **Writing coach**.

## 6. Download your university reports

Go to **University reports**:

- **Excel report:** summary, programs and requirements with your eligibility, ranked professors, a checklist with drop-downs, and your profile.
- **Word report:** a readable report to print or share with a mentor.
- **Excel: all universities:** one file with every university, program and professor.

## Privacy in one line

Your CV, scores and emails stay in your browser. There is no account and no Gradfessor server. **Backup** saves a copy to your computer; **Delete my data** erases everything.

## Workshop checklist (for presenters)

- Install the extension on the presentation laptop the day before and run one department scan (Wi-Fi can be slow on the day).
- Demo order: Start here → Upload CV → Scan a department → Capture requirements → Draft email → University reports.
- Ask students to bring their CV as a PDF and one target department link.
- Collect feedback with a short anonymous form (what helped, what broke, would you recommend it).
